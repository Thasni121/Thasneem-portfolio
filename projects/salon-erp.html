Form & Launch — Salon ERP Web Application.

ABOUT THE WORK / 02

What the documentation tells us.

The portfolio document describes Form & Launch as a salon management web application designed to streamline daily operations through a centralized digital platform. It supports customer information, appointments, salon services, staff activities, billing, inventory, and business operations.

Key features & contributions

  • Appointment and booking management to organize customer appointments and salon service scheduling.
  • Customer profiles, service history, and relevant customer information.
  • Billing workflows and transaction management.
  • Staff information, responsibilities, and service-related activities.
  • Salon product and inventory records, including stock availability.
  • A centralized business dashboard and reports for a view of salon operations and performance.
  • User-friendly interface designed to simplify everyday tasks and improve operational efficiency.
